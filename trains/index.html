Trains

Basel sits where Switzerland, Germany and France meet — and the three rail networks meet here too. Use the trains for trips out of the city and for fast cross-river hops on the regional S-Bahn.

Three stations to know

  • Basel SBBThe main Swiss station. Trams 1, 2, 8, 10, 11 stop right outside.
  • Basel Bad BfThe German Deutsche Bahn station, north of the Rhine in Kleinbasel.
  • Basel SNCFThe French side, sharing the Basel SBB building. Different platforms, different border.

What runs on the rails

Three layers of train services use these stations:

  • Regio S-Bahn Basel — the trinational regional network, connecting Basel with neighbouring towns in Switzerland, south Baden (Germany) and Alsace (France). Frequent, included in the TNW fare for journeys inside Basel.
  • Swiss intercity (IC, IR) — fast, frequent trains to Zürich, Bern, Geneva, Lucerne, Olten and beyond, run by SBB.
  • International rail — TGV Lyria to Paris, ICE/EC to Frankfurt, Hamburg and Berlin, plus regional connections to Mulhouse, Freiburg im Breisgau, Strasbourg and Karlsruhe.

When to take a train

  • Day trips to other Swiss cities (Zürich is under an hour).
  • Cross-border excursions to Freiburg, Mulhouse or Colmar.
  • Commuting in from a town along the S-Bahn corridor.
  • Long-distance international travel without a flight.

When something else is better

  • Inside the city core — the tram is more direct.
  • To the airport — bus 50 is faster than any train option from Basel SBB.
  • Crossing the Rhine for the experience — a ferry beats a train every time.

Tickets & tips

  • Local trains in BaselCovered by your TNW ticket within the relevant zones
  • Long-distance Swiss trainsBuy via SBB Mobile, online or at any ticket machine
  • Discount cardsHalf-Fare Card, GA Travelcard, saver day passes
  • ReservationsOptional within Switzerland; recommended for TGV/ICE
If your train leaves from Basel Bad Bf or the SNCF section, leave extra time. The walk from the SBB main hall to the SNCF platforms crosses customs, and Basel Bad Bf is across the river.

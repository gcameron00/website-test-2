Tickets & resources

Everything below points to an official source. Fares, schedules and apps change — always confirm with the operator before relying on anything here.

One ticket, most of the network

Local journeys in and around Basel are covered by the TNW (Tarifverbund Nordwestschweiz). One ticket, valid for a defined number of fare zones and a defined time, gets you on every tram, bus and S-Bahn train within those zones — no matter the operator.

  • Single ticketValid for one journey within the bought zones, time-limited (typically ~60 min within zone 10)
  • Day ticketUnlimited travel for one calendar day within the bought zones
  • 9-o'clock day ticketCheaper, valid Monday–Friday from 09:00 and all day on weekends
  • Multi-journey cardBooklet of pre-paid journeys at a small discount
  • U-Abo / monthlyFor residents and longer stays

Free travel for hotel guests

If you stay overnight in a registered Basel hotel, hostel or campsite, you receive a Mobility Ticket at check-in. It gives you free unlimited travel on the BVB and BLT trams and buses, and on second-class regional S-Bahn within Basel, for the duration of your stay.

Apps to install before you arrive

  • SBB Mobile — the official Swiss railway app. Plans journeys end-to-end, including trams, sells almost every ticket you need. sbb.ch/apps
  • BVB — Basel city operator app. Tickets and live timetables for the city network. bvb.ch
  • BLT — Baselland operator app, useful if you live or travel beyond the city. blt.ch
  • PubliBike — to unlock shared bikes. publibike.ch
  • Donkey Republic — alternative bike share. donkey.bike

Fare zones

The TNW area is divided into numbered zones. Most of the city of Basel sits inside zone 10. Trips to suburbs and surrounding villages cross into adjacent zones. The TNW website has the authoritative zone map and fare table:

  • tnw.ch — fares, zones, conditions

Accessibility

  • Most BVB and BLT trams are low-floor and step-free.
  • Train stations have lifts; SBB publishes accessibility info per station at sbb.ch.
  • Ferries vary — call ahead if you need step-free boarding.
  • The Swiss "Mobility for All" service plans assisted journeys nationwide.

Lost & found

  • BVB lost & found — see bvb.ch
  • SBB lost & found — see sbb.ch
  • For ferries, contact the operator listed on faehri.ch

Disruption & live status

For real-time disruptions, check the operator's home page or app — BVB, BLT and SBB all publish service alerts there. Twitter/X is no longer a reliable channel for any of them.

About

About this site

A short, plain-language guide to public transport in Basel, Switzerland — the kind of overview a friendly local would give you on day one.

What this is

An independent, framework-free static site that explains the modes of public transport available in Basel and points to the official apps and websites that you'll actually use to plan and pay for journeys.

What this isn't

  • A live timetable. The operators do that better.
  • A ticket vendor. We send you to the official channels.
  • A trip planner or routing service.
  • An official voice for any operator.

Why bother?

Visitors and newcomers often arrive in Basel and try to piece the picture together from a dozen tabs: BVB, BLT, SBB, TNW, faehri.ch, the Mobility Ticket, the airport bus, PubliBike, the night network… Each source is excellent at its own corner of the puzzle, but nobody puts them next to each other in a single, neutral overview. That's the gap this site tries to fill.

How it's built

Plain HTML, CSS and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no tracking. Hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The repository is the site — anyone can read the source, propose a change, or fork it.

Corrections and contributions

Did a fare change? An operator merge? A link rot? Open a pull request against the relevant page on GitHub. Please keep edits factual and link to an official source.

Disclaimers

  • This site is not affiliated with BVB, BLT, SBB, TNW, PubliBike, Donkey Republic or any other operator.
  • Operator names, logos and route numbers belong to their respective owners.
  • Information is provided in good faith but without warranty. Always check the official source before travelling.

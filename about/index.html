About Visit Marbella

A small, independent guide for people who have a few days on the western Costa del Sol and want to spend that time well.

What this site is

Visit Marbella is a focused tourist's guide to one place on the Costa del Sol. It's written for people who have between a long weekend and a full week, and it concentrates on what they're most likely to want — culture, food, shopping, beach time, and day trips — rather than where to sleep or how to buy a holiday flat.

The brief is biased toward the off-season (especially the first half of May), when the weather is mild, restaurant terraces are open, and the Old Town isn't yet shoulder-to-shoulder.

How recommendations are chosen

  • First-hand experience. If something is recommended, it's because we've been recently and would go back.
  • No paid placements. No restaurant, hotel, tour, or shop has paid to be here, and none ever will.
  • No affiliate links. External links go to the venue's own site or to a neutral source like a transport operator.
  • Honest about trade-offs. We mention queues, parking, and accessibility, even when the place is otherwise great.
  • Time-stamped facts. Where opening hours, prices, or transport details appear, we tag them with the month they were verified.

What this site is not

  • It is not a hotel listings site. By design.
  • It is not a booking engine. It links out to operators where relevant.
  • It is not a real-estate or relocation site. There are plenty of those already.
  • It is not exhaustive. We'd rather say less, more accurately, than try to cover everything.

Suggest an edit or report a problem

The site is open source. If a restaurant has closed, an opening time has changed, a bus route has moved, or you want to suggest something we've missed, the easiest thing to do is open an issue or a pull request on the repository.

  • Open an issue
  • View the source on GitHub

A note on the tech

This site is plain HTML, CSS and a few lines of vanilla JavaScript. There's no framework, no build step, and no third-party scripts beyond Cloudflare's own analytics on the host. Everything you see is in the repository as-is. That keeps it fast, accessible, and cheap to run.

Disclaimer
